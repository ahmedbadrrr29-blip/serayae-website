Being someone's guardian

A guardian is one of the few people SERAYAE contacts when the person who chose them raises an alert. Not a follower, not a supervisor. Someone who will pick up.

Being named is not the same as agreeing. Until you confirm the invite from the phone number it was sent to, you are not counted, and the person who added you is told plainly that you are not yet reachable — because an emergency contact who has not agreed is not an emergency contact.

What you see, and what you never see

You see

  • An alert, the moment they raise one
  • Their location while that alert is open, so you can reach them
  • Their name, and whatever note they chose to send
  • When the alert ends, and how it ended

You never see

  • Where they are at any other time
  • Their movements, history, or routine
  • Their other guardians' numbers
  • Their location, at any point, unless you have confirmed and they have raised an alert

It does not run the other way either. Accepting does not share your location with them, and SERAYAE does not track you for agreeing to help.

How it works, start to finish

  1. They add you by name and number, and you get one invitation message. If they call for help before you have confirmed anything, an emergency SMS can still reach the number they saved — that is what saving an emergency contact means, and it is the one thing that does not wait for the app.
  2. You confirm in the app, signed in with that same number. That is the step that lets you see where they are during an alert, and the step their screen counts when it tells them how many guardians can be reached. Only you can take it — and once SERAYAE is released, the app will ask you to.
  3. Nothing happens — hopefully for a very long time. There is no check-in, no notification, no reason to open the app.
  4. They raise an alert. You are contacted, and you can see where they are for as long as it is open.
  5. You step away whenever you want, from the app. Their own screen then shows one fewer guardian reachable, so they can name someone else. Nobody is left believing in cover they do not have.
Why confirmation is required at all: an unconfirmed number is a promise nobody made. If SERAYAE treated a typed-in number as a guardian, someone could go out believing four people had their back when the alert was quietly going nowhere.

If you would rather not

Decline in the app, or simply do nothing — an invite that is never accepted grants nothing and expires on its own. You are not told off for it, and the person who invited you sees only that the number is not reachable, so they can ask someone else. Saying no early is more useful to them than saying yes and not meaning it.

Questions we get asked

Does it cost me anything? No. Being a guardian is free, and always will be.

Do I need the app open? No. You are contacted whether or not the app is running.

Can I be a guardian for more than one person? Yes, for as many as ask you.

What if I miss an alert? Others are contacted too, and the alert stays open until it is resolved. Nothing rests on one phone being picked up.
