Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026 · Applies to the SERAYAE app (formerly Solra) and this website

SERAYAE is a personal safety application. SERAYAE is not an emergency service and does not replace your local emergency services — 122/123/180 in Egypt, 999 in the UK, 911 in the US and Canada, 112 in India and the EU. In life-threatening situations, always call them first.

1. Who we are

SERAYAE (formerly Solra) (“we”, “our”, “us”) is operated from Cairo, Egypt. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data when you use the SERAYAE mobile application and this website, and the rights you have over that data.

2. What SERAYAE does with your safety

SERAYAE helps you reach the people who can help you: your trusted contacts (guardians) and nearby members of the SERAYAE community. It assists you in contacting help. It does not dispatch police, ambulance, or fire services, and it does not guarantee that anyone will respond or arrive.

3. Data we collect

Account data
Phone number, name, email (optional), gender (optional).
Precise location
GPS coordinates — collected only during active emergencies and Safe Walk sessions you start, and never continuously in the background outside those sessions. Never used for advertising. Never sold.
Medical profile (optional)
Blood type, allergies, medications, medical conditions, emergency notes — provided by you, shared with responders only when you trigger an SOS.
Trusted contacts
Names and phone numbers of the guardians you choose.
Emergency records and evidence
Records of emergency events, including their location, timeline, and any evidence you capture (photos, audio, video). During an active emergency, live voice/video sessions may be carried through our real-time provider.
Responder verification data
If you apply to become a responder: a photograph of your government ID and a selfie, used to review your identity. Stored securely and accessed through time-limited signed links.
Family safety data
If a parent or legal guardian enables family features: the child’s name, live location during monitored sessions, safe zones, and related alerts — processed only under the parent’s or guardian’s consent and control.
Device and diagnostic data
Device type, OS version, app version, and error diagnostics used for troubleshooting and reliability.
Waitlist data (this website)
Your email address and language preference — used only to send launch updates. Nothing else.

4. Device permissions we request

The app asks for each permission only when the related feature needs it, and explains why before asking:

Location (precise)
To share your position during SOS and Safe Walk sessions and to alert nearby members. During an active session, location may continue updating while the app is in the background, and stops when the session ends.
Camera and microphone
To capture evidence you choose to record and to carry live voice/video during active emergencies.
Notifications
To deliver alerts when someone near you needs help, and updates about your own sessions.

You can revoke any permission in your device settings at any time; related features will stop working, but the rest of the app remains usable.

5. How we use your data

  • Alerting nearby available community members and your trusted contacts when you trigger an SOS.
  • Sharing your live location with those alerted — during active emergencies and Safe Walk sessions only.
  • Sharing your medical information with responders when you trigger an SOS.
  • Sending verification codes via SMS to sign you in.
  • Generating anonymized incident maps — locations are aggregated and small clusters are suppressed so individuals are never shown.
  • Reviewing responder applications.
  • Operating family safety features for accounts that enable them.
  • Diagnosing errors and keeping the service reliable.
  • Sending launch updates to the email you gave us on the waitlist.

6. Service providers we share data with

We share data with the following processors, only to operate the service:

Twilio and Akedly
SMS delivery for sign-in verification codes.
Google Maps
Map display, geocoding, and walking routes.
Firebase (Google)
Push notifications for alerts.
Agora
Live voice and video sessions during active emergencies.
Cloudinary
Secure storage of evidence media and responder verification images, accessed via expiring signed links.
Sentry
Crash and error diagnostics; reports may include device and request metadata.
RevenueCat
Subscription management, if and when you purchase a subscription.
Railway
Cloud hosting of our backend (United States).
Supabase
Waitlist storage for this website (European Union).
YouTube (Google)
The optional music player on this website streams from YouTube; YouTube sets its own cookies only if you choose to play it.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers.

7. International transfers

Our backend is hosted in the United States, so data you provide to the app is transferred to and processed there. We apply the safeguards described in this policy to all such processing and are working with counsel to maintain compliance with the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law and, where applicable, the GDPR and UK GDPR.

8. Security

All communication with our servers uses HTTPS/TLS. Access to your data requires authenticated, revocable tokens; sensitive media is reachable only through expiring signed links; and our APIs enforce rate limiting and input validation. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim ours is — we design so that a single failure does not expose your data.

9. Data retention

  • Safe Walk location points are automatically deleted 90 days after the walk ends.
  • Emergency records, including their locations and evidence, are retained while your account is active, for your safety history and legal purposes, and are deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Account and medical profile data are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Waitlist emails are deleted on request, and after launch communications end.

10. Your rights

Under the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law and, where applicable, the GDPR, UK GDPR, and other local data-protection laws, you have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, delete your account and data, and export your data in a portable format. Account deletion is available directly in the app and on the web, and removes your personal data as described there.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process your data on these legal bases: your consent (optional features such as the medical profile and the waitlist), performance of our contract with you (operating your account and sessions), protection of vital interests (processing needed during an emergency), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and reliable. You may withdraw consent at any time, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

11. Children

SERAYAE accounts require you to be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children, with one exception: a parent or legal guardian may enable family safety features for their own child, in which case the child’s data described in Section 3 is processed under that parent’s or guardian’s consent and control, and is deleted with the parent’s account.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes through the app and update the date at the top of this page.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact support@serayae.me.
