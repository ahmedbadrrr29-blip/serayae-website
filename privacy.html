Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

SERAYAE (formerly Solra) (“we”, “our”, “us”) is an emergency response mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use SERAYAE. It applies to the SERAYAE mobile app and this website.

1. Data we collect

Account data
Phone number, name, email (optional), gender (optional).
Location data
GPS coordinates — collected only during active emergencies and Walk With Me sessions, never continuously.
Medical profile
Blood type, allergies, medications, medical conditions, emergency notes — stored encrypted.
Trusted contacts
Names and phone numbers of your emergency contacts.
Emergency history
Records of past emergency events, kept for safety and legal purposes.
Device data
Device type, OS version, and app version, used for troubleshooting.
Waitlist data (this website)
Your email address and language preference, used only to send launch updates. Nothing else.

2. How we use your data

  • Matching you with nearby responders during emergencies.
  • Sending OTP verification codes via SMS.
  • Sharing your location with responders during active emergencies only.
  • Generating anonymized incident heatmaps — your identity is never shown.
  • Sharing medical information with emergency responders when you trigger SOS.
  • Sending launch updates to the email you gave us on the waitlist.

3. Third-party services

Twilio
SMS delivery for OTP verification.
Google Maps
Map display and location services.
Firebase
Push notifications for emergency alerts.
Railway
Cloud hosting for our backend servers (US-based).
Supabase
Waitlist storage for this website (EU-based).

4. Data security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted database connections, JWT token authentication, HTTPS for all communications, and rate limiting to prevent abuse. Medical data is stored with additional encryption.

5. Your rights

Under the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law and GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

6. Data retention

Emergency records are retained for legal and safety purposes. Account data is deleted upon request. Location data from completed emergencies is anonymized after 90 days. Waitlist emails are deleted on request and after launch communications end.

7. Children’s privacy

SERAYAE is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Contact us

For privacy concerns, contact us at privacy@solraapp.net.
