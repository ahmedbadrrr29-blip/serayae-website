Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Applies to the Serayae app and this website · العربية

Serayae is a health companion and personal safety app. Serayae is not an emergency service and does not replace your local emergency services — 122/123/180 in Egypt, 999 in the UK, 911 in the US and Canada, 112 in India and the EU. In life-threatening situations, always call them first. Serayae AI is not a doctor and can make mistakes.

1. Who we are

Serayae (“we”, “our”, “us”) is operated from Cairo, Egypt. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data when you use the Serayae mobile application and this website, and the rights you have over that data.

2. What Serayae does

Serayae helps you keep track of your health — symptoms, medicines, and how you feel — and helps you reach the people who can help you when you are not safe: the safety contacts you choose and, where available, nearby members of the Serayae community. It does not dispatch police, ambulance, or fire services, and it does not guarantee that anyone will respond or arrive.

3. Data we collect

Account data
Your name, email address, and date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or older). You sign in with Apple, with Google, or with your email, a password, and a 6-digit code we email to you. Gender is optional.
Phone number (optional)
You can add a phone number in the app. We do not verify it by text message. It is used only so a safety contact or a doctor can call you back, and to match invites someone sent to that number.
Health data (special category)
Only if you give explicit consent: the conditions you choose, symptoms you log and how severe they are, possible triggers, medicines, doses you take, tablets left and refills, mood and energy check-ins, allergies, and emergency notes. You can use Serayae’s safety features without giving any health data.
Serayae AI
If you turn Serayae AI on: the messages you write and its replies, and, only while “Remember things from chats” is on, short notes it keeps to help next time (at most 50, never a name, number, email, link, or address).
Precise location
GPS coordinates — collected only during an SOS and journeys you start, and never continuously in the background outside those. Trips you save for one-tap journeys keep the name, address, and point you saved. Never used for advertising. Never sold.
Safety contacts
Names and phone numbers of the people you invite, whether they accepted, and which of them you picked for each journey.
Emergency records and evidence
Records of emergency events, including their location, timeline, and any evidence you capture (photos, audio, video). During an active emergency, live voice/video sessions may be carried through our real-time provider.
Identity verification data
If you choose to verify your identity: a photo of your government ID and a live selfie. Verification is needed only to post in Community, report an area on the safety map, or help others nearby. SOS never needs it. Stored securely and accessed through time-limited signed links.
Family safety data
If a parent or legal guardian enables family features: the child’s name, live location during monitored sessions, safe zones, and related alerts — processed only under the parent’s or guardian’s consent and control.
Device and diagnostic data
Device type, OS version, app version, and error diagnostics used for troubleshooting and reliability. Diagnostics never include health data or Serayae AI messages.
Waitlist data (this website)
Your email address and language preference — used only to send launch updates. Nothing else.

4. Device permissions we request

The app asks for each permission only when the related feature needs it, and explains why before asking:

Location (precise)
To share your position during an SOS or a journey and to alert nearby members. During an active SOS or journey, location may keep updating while the app is in the background, and stops when it ends.
Camera and microphone
To capture evidence you choose to record, to carry live voice/video during active emergencies, and to take your ID photo and selfie if you choose to verify.
Notifications
To deliver alerts when someone near you or someone you look out for needs help, medicine reminders, and updates about your own sessions.

You can revoke any permission in your device settings at any time; related features will stop working, but the rest of the app remains usable.

5. How we use your data

  • Alerting your safety contacts in the app, and nearby available community members, when you trigger an SOS or a journey runs late.
  • Sharing your live location with those alerted — during an SOS or a journey only.
  • Sharing your medical information with responders when you trigger an SOS.
  • Showing your health logs, patterns, reminders, and reports back to you.
  • Answering you in Serayae AI, if you turned it on.
  • Telling a safety contact you are having a hard day, only when you tap to send it. The message does not include your symptoms or medicines.
  • Emailing a 6-digit code to sign you in.
  • Sending a one-time code by WhatsApp or SMS to someone you invited, so they can accept the invite.
  • Generating anonymized incident maps — locations are aggregated and small clusters are suppressed so individuals are never shown.
  • Reviewing identity verification.
  • Operating family safety features for accounts that enable them.
  • Diagnosing errors and keeping the service reliable.
  • Sending launch updates to the email you gave us on the waitlist.

Our staff tools are built so that staff cannot see your health data or your Serayae AI messages.

6. Service providers we share data with

We share data with the following processors, only to operate the service:

Google Gemini API
Writes Serayae AI’s replies. We use Google’s paid tier, under terms in which Google does not use what we send to train its models. Before a message is sent, names and numbers are removed. A message that suggests you may be in crisis is never sent; Serayae answers it with help lines directly.
Apple and Google
Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, if you choose them; they confirm who you are and share your name and email with us.
Resend
Sends sign-in codes, the waitlist welcome, and launch emails from our own domain (European Union).
Akedly
Delivers one-time codes by WhatsApp or SMS to people you invite as safety contacts.
Google Maps
Map display, geocoding, and walking routes.
Firebase (Google)
Push notifications for alerts and reminders.
Agora
Live voice and video sessions during active emergencies.
Cloudinary
Secure storage of evidence media and identity verification images, accessed via expiring signed links.
Sentry
Crash and error diagnostics; reports may include device and request metadata, never health data.
RevenueCat
Subscription management, if and when you purchase a subscription.
Heroku (Salesforce)
Cloud hosting of our backend and its database, provisioned in the European Union (Ireland). Some operational functions of the platform — such as database backups and diagnostic tooling — may be processed in the United States.
Supabase
Waitlist storage for this website (European Union).
Cloudflare
DNS, email routing, and privacy-first website analytics for serayae.me — cookieless and aggregate only; no individual visitors are tracked.
YouTube (Google)
The optional music player on this website streams from YouTube; YouTube sets its own cookies only if you choose to play it.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers.

7. International transfers

Our backend and its database are provisioned in the European Union (Ireland). Because you may be located elsewhere — including Egypt — data you provide to the app is transferred to and processed there. We cannot promise that every operational function of our hosting platform stays inside the European Union: platform-level features such as database backups and diagnostic tooling may be handled in the United States.

Several of the processors listed above operate outside the European Union, including in the United States, so specific categories of data reach those countries too: what you write to Serayae AI, with names and numbers removed, when a reply is written; an invited person’s phone number when a code is sent; your location when a map or route is drawn; your device token when a push notification is delivered; evidence media you choose to record; and diagnostic metadata when the app reports an error. We apply the safeguards described in this policy to all such processing and are working with counsel to maintain compliance with the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law and, where applicable, the GDPR and UK GDPR.

8. Security

All communication with our servers uses HTTPS/TLS. Serayae AI messages and memories are encrypted in our database. Access to your data requires authenticated, revocable tokens; sensitive media is reachable only through expiring signed links; and our APIs enforce rate limiting and input validation. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim ours is — we design so that a single failure does not expose your data.

9. Data retention

  • Serayae AI messages are deleted 30 days after they are written. Its notes are kept until you delete them or turn “Remember things from chats” off.
  • Journey location points are automatically deleted 90 days after the journey ends. Saved trips are kept until you delete them.
  • Health data is kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Emergency records, including their locations and evidence, are retained while your account is active, for your safety history and legal purposes, and are deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Account data is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Waitlist emails are deleted on request, and after launch communications end.

10. Your rights

Under the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law and, where applicable, the GDPR, UK GDPR, and other local data-protection laws, you have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, delete your account and data, and export your data in a portable format. Account deletion is available directly in the app and on the web, and removes your personal data as described there.

Health data is a special category of personal data. We process it only with your explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), which you give in the app before any health data is saved, and separately before you use Serayae AI. During an emergency, we may share your medical information with responders to protect your vital interests (Article 9(2)(c)). You can withdraw either consent at any time, in the app or by emailing support@serayae.me; we then stop processing that data for that purpose.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process your other data on these legal bases: your consent (optional features such as the waitlist), performance of our contract with you (operating your account and sessions), protection of vital interests (processing needed during an emergency), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and reliable. You may withdraw consent at any time, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

11. Children

Serayae accounts require you to be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children, with one exception: a parent or legal guardian may enable family safety features for their own child, in which case the child’s data described in Section 3 is processed under that parent’s or guardian’s consent and control, and is deleted with the parent’s account.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes through the app and update the date at the top of this page.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact support@serayae.me.
