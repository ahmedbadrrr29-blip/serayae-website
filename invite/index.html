Guardian invite

Someone asked you to be their guardian.

They put your number on SERAYAE as one of the people to contact if something goes wrong. Nothing has been shared with you yet, and nothing will be until you confirm it is you.

Confirming happens in the app, signed in with this phone number — the number the invite was sent to. That is deliberate: it is the only way to be sure a link forwarded to somebody else cannot make a stranger into their guardian.

What happens next

  1. Open SERAYAE on this phone — install it first if you do not have it.
  2. Sign in with this number. You will get a one-time code by SMS.
  3. Accept the invite. Only then does the person who invited you count you as reachable.
