Guardian invite

Someone asked you to be their guardian.

They put your number on SERAYAE as one of the people to contact if something goes wrong. Nothing about them has been shared with you, and you will not see their location unless they raise an alert and you have confirmed it is you. Your number is already saved as an emergency contact, though — so if they call for help before you confirm anything, an emergency SMS can still reach you at it.

Confirming happens in the app, signed in with this phone number — the number the invite was sent to. That is deliberate: it is the only way to be sure a link forwarded to somebody else cannot make a stranger into their guardian.

اختارك أحدهم وليّ أمان له على سراي. لم يُشارَك معك أي شيء عنه، ولن ترَي موقعه إلا إذا أطلق نداء استغاثة وأكّدتِ أن هذا الرقم رقمك. لكن رقمك محفوظ عنده بالفعل كرقم للطوارئ، فإذا طلب المساعدة قبل أن تؤكّدي أي شيء قد تصلك رسالة استغاثة عليه.

التأكيد يحدث داخل التطبيق، بتسجيل الدخول بهذا الرقم نفسه — الرقم الذي وصلتك عليه الدعوة. وهذا مقصود: هي الطريقة الوحيدة لضمان أن رابطًا أُعيد إرساله لشخص آخر لا يجعل غريبًا وليّ أمان.

لا شيء مطلوب منك اليوم: سراي لم تُنشر في متاجر التطبيقات بعد، فلا يوجد تطبيق على هذا الهاتف لتفتحيه، وهذه الصفحة لا تطلب منك ما لا تستطيع الوفاء به.

What happens next

  1. Nothing, today. SERAYAE is not in the app stores yet, so there is no app on this phone for you to open — and this page does not ask you to do anything it cannot deliver.
  2. When it is released, you will be asked to confirm in the app, signed in with this number. A one-time code by SMS proves the number is yours.
  3. Only that confirmation lets them see your location during an alert, and only then does the app count you as a reachable guardian.

If you did not want this

Ignoring this invite is enough on its own — an invite that is never confirmed grants nothing and expires by itself in 14 days. If you would rather not be asked again, the button below tells us to stop sending guardian invitations to this number, whoever adds it.
