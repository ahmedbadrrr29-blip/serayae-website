Guardian invite

Someone asked you to be their guardian.

They put your number on SERAYAE as one of the people to contact if something goes wrong. Nothing about them has been shared with you, and you will not see their location unless they raise an alert and you have confirmed it is you. Until you accept, an SOS from them will not be sent to you at all — SERAYAE only contacts guardians who have confirmed their own number, which is why they were asked to invite you.

You can accept right here, on this page. It takes a one-time code by SMS to this phone number — the number the invite was sent to. That is deliberate: proving the number is the only way to be sure a link forwarded to somebody else cannot make a stranger into their guardian.

Accepting means one thing: if they send an SOS, SERAYAE will contact you at this number. It does not start you following anybody, it does not share your location, and there is nothing to watch — you will see nothing at all until there is an emergency.

اختارك أحدهم وليّ أمان له على سراي. لم يُشارَك معك أي شيء عنه، ولن ترَي موقعه إلا إذا أطلق نداء استغاثة وأكّدتِ أن هذا الرقم رقمك. واعلمي أنه قبل قبولك لن يُرسل إليك نداء الاستغاثة منه إطلاقًا — فسراي لا تتصل إلا بمن أكّدوا أرقامهم، ولهذا أُرسلت لك الدعوة.

اقبلي الدعوة من هذه الصفحة مباشرة. سيصلك رمز لمرة واحدة برسالة نصية على هذا الرقم — الرقم الذي وصلتك عليه الدعوة. وهذا مقصود: إثبات الرقم هو الطريقة الوحيدة لضمان أن رابطًا أُعيد إرساله لشخص آخر لا يجعل غريبًا وليّ أمان.

اعرفي معنى القبول: عند إرسال نداء استغاثة، ستتصل بك سراي على هذا الرقم. لا تتابعين موقع أحد، ولا يُشارَك موقعك، ولا يوجد شيء لمراقبته — لن ترَي شيئًا حتى تحدث حالة طوارئ.

Accept the invitation

The number this message arrived on. Any other number will be refused — the invitation is tied to this one.

اكتبي الرقم الذي وصلتك عليه هذه الرسالة. أي رقم آخر سيُرفَض — الدعوة مرتبطة بهذا الرقم وحده.

Confirming the code signs you in to SERAYAE, and creates an account for this number if you do not already have one — that is how the invitation can be checked against your number rather than against a link anyone could forward. This page keeps nothing: no code, no token and no sign-in is saved on this device.

تأكيد الرمز يُسجّل دخولك إلى سراي، ويُنشئ حسابًا لهذا الرقم إن لم يكن لديك حساب — بهذه الطريقة تُفحَص الدعوة مقابل رقمك، لا مقابل رابط يمكن لأي شخص إعادة إرساله. ولا تحفظ هذه الصفحة شيئًا على هذا الجهاز: لا الرمز ولا الرابط ولا تسجيل الدخول.

What happens next

  1. Accept above with the number this message reached. A one-time code by SMS proves the number is yours; nothing is confirmed without it.
  2. After that, an SOS from the person who invited you will be sent to this number. Until then it will not — an invitation that is never accepted counts for nothing, which is exactly what their app tells them.
  3. Only that confirmation lets them share their location with you during an alert. Nothing is shared before it, and nothing is shared between alerts.
  4. If you do have the app, you can accept there instead — the button below opens it. SERAYAE is not in the app stores yet, so for almost everyone this page is the way.

If you did not want this

Ignoring this invite is enough on its own — an invite that is never confirmed grants nothing and expires by itself in 14 days. If you would rather not be asked again, the button below tells us to stop sending guardian invitations to this number, whoever adds it.
