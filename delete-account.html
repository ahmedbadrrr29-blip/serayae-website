Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 13, 2026 · Applies to the SERAYAE app and this website

You can delete your SERAYAE account and personal data at any time. Deletion is permanent.

Delete in the app

Open SERAYAE → Settings → Account → Delete Account, and confirm. Your account and personal data are removed as described below.

Delete on the web

If you no longer have the app installed, request deletion by emailing support@serayae.me from the email or phone number on your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile, and medical profile.
  • Your trusted contacts and guardian links.
  • Your Safe Walk history and location data.
  • Your emergency records, evidence media, and family safety data.
  • Responder verification images, if you applied.

Some records may be retained where the law requires it — for example, records relevant to an ongoing legal proceeding.

Waitlist emails

To remove your email from the pre-launch waitlist, write to support@serayae.me.
